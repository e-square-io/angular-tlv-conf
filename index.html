Habima National Theatre, Tel Aviv

Located in the heart of Tel Aviv, Habima Theatre is Israel's national theatre and a cultural landmark. Situated at Habima Square, it offers a central and prestigious venue for performances, conferences, and events. With state-of-the-art acoustics, multimedia, and lighting systems, Habima hosts top-tier productions, concerts, and international events, making it a prime destination for arts and entertainment in Israel.

Habima offers accessibility services such as: parking, designated seating areas, and tailored audio systems.
